Before You Proceed

This application is a reference tool only. Information is based on manufacturer service manuals, industry best practices, and general marine engineering guidelines. It is not a substitute for official manufacturer documentation.

Always verify part numbers against the current manufacturer parts catalog and the specific vessel's equipment serial numbers before ordering. Part numbers are subject to change and may vary by engine arrangement, model year, or vessel configuration.

All maintenance, repairs, and inspections must be performed safely and in accordance with applicable U.S. Coast Guard regulations, company policies and procedures, and any governing federal, state, or local requirements.

In any emergency, follow established emergency procedures and contact the appropriate authorities. This app does not replace proper training, a valid Certificate of Inspection, or qualified engineering judgment.
